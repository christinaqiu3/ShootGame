# ShootGame

This is a fast-paced 3D vertical platformer where the player must use a grappling hook, climbing mechanics, and quick reflexes to race to the top of a hostile city — all while being hunted by a teleporting sniper. The only way to survive is to keep moving and outplay your enemy.

Your goal is simple: reach the sniper and take them down before they kill you first.

### Player Mechanics

* Left Mouse Click — Shoot bullets to damage the enemy
* Right Mouse Click — Use the grappling hook to swing between platforms
* Tab — Toggle between two different platform types
* K (near tall surfaces) — Enter climbing mode, then use WASD to climb
 * Be careful: moving platforms and attacks can knock you off!

### Enemy Mechanics

* Sniper enemy constantly fires red and blue bullets
* Sniper teleports randomly between platforms above
* The sniper switches between randomized attack styles:
 * Fast light attacks
 * Slower but heavy barrages
* Goal: Reach the sniper and eliminate them to win!
